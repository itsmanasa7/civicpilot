# PM-KISAN Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## FAQ 1: What is the PM-KISAN Scheme and who finances it?
PM-KISAN is a central sector welfare scheme launched by the Government of India. The entire financial liability towards transfer of benefits is borne 100% by the Central Government of India without any state funding contribution.

## FAQ 2: How many installments are paid in a financial year?
Eligible farmers receive a total of Rs. 6,000 per financial year divided into three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 each. The periods of disbursement are: Period 1 (April to July), Period 2 (August to November), and Period 3 (December to March).

## FAQ 3: How can a farmer check their beneficiary status and payment ledger?
Farmers can check their beneficiary status and installment credit details by visiting the official PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) and accessing the 'Know Your Status' module using their registration number or registered mobile number.

## FAQ 4: Can tenant farmers or sharecroppers claim benefits?
No. Tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and landless agricultural laborers are not eligible under the PM-KISAN scheme. The scheme strictly requires ownership of cultivable land registered in the state revenue records.

## FAQ 5: What should a farmer do if their Aadhaar is not seeded with the bank account?
Beneficiary farmers must visit their local bank branch or postal payment bank (IPPB) to link and seed their Aadhaar number with their bank account via NPCI mapping. Without active Aadhaar seeding, subsequent DBT installments cannot be credited.

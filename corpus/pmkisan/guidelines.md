# PM-KISAN Operational Scheme Guidelines

## Clause 1.0: Introduction and Scheme Objectives
The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme with 100% funding from the Government of India. The scheme aims to augment the income of all landholding farmer families across the country to procure various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

## Clause 2.0: Definition of Farmer Family
A landholder farmer family is defined as a family comprising of husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as per land records of the concerned State or Union Territory.

## Clause 3.0: Benefit Amount and Installment Schedule
Under the scheme, financial assistance of Rs. 6,000/- per annum is provided to eligible beneficiary farmer families. The benefit is transferred directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries in three equal 4-monthly installments of Rs. 2,000/- each, covering April-July, August-November, and December-March periods.

## Clause 4.1: Identification of Eligible Beneficiary Families
The responsibility of identifying the eligible beneficiary farmer families and uploading their particulars on the PM-KISAN portal rests entirely with the respective State and Union Territory Governments.

## Clause 4.2: Eligibility Criteria for Landholding Farmers
All landholding farmer families who hold cultivable landholding in their names are eligible to receive benefits under the PM-KISAN scheme, subject to the exclusion criteria defined in Clause 5.0. Ownership of the land parcel is verified against the State Revenue Department records or digitized RoR (Records of Right).

## Clause 5.0: Exclusion Categories
The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status shall not be eligible for benefits under the scheme:
1. All Institutional Landholders.
2. Farmer families in which one or more members belong to prescribed exclusion categories such as constitutional post holders, current/former ministers, civil servants, and professionals.

## Clause 5.1: Institutional and Pensioner Exclusions
Under Clause 5.1, institutional landholders are strictly excluded from PM-KISAN. Additionally, all retired government pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000/- or more (excluding multi-tasking staff / Class IV / Group D employees) are ineligible to receive financial assistance.

## Clause 6.0: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Mechanism
All financial payments under the PM-KISAN scheme are disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of the beneficiary farmers through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) and National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge.

## Clause 7.0: Incorrect Declarations and Recovery Procedure
If a beneficiary is found to have provided false declarations or received funds despite falling into the exclusion criteria, the entire transferred amount shall be recovered through legal proceedings and revenue recovery measures initiated by the State Government.
